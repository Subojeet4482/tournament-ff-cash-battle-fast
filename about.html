1Who we are

FF Cash Battle (ffcashbattle.in) is an independent Free Fire tournament platform for players in India. Players join daily CS 4v4 and Battle Royale custom-room matches, pay a small entry fee, and prize money goes into the in-app wallet.

We are not related to "Clash Battle", "FF Clash Battle" or any similar-sounding site. The only official domain is ffcashbattle.in.

3How payments work

  • Deposit: pay by UPI and submit the amount with your UTR / Transaction ID. Deposits are verified and credited to your Deposit Balance.
  • Withdrawal: winnings go to your Withdrawal Balance. Withdraw by UPI or as a Google Play gift code.
  • Track everything: every deposit, entry fee, prize and withdrawal is listed in your wallet history with an order number.
  • Track record: 27+ withdrawals paid in the first 13 days. Withdrawals started on 2 November 2026. At least 2 payments were made every day from 2 to 15 November. Any amount (Rs 10, Rs 1000 or more) will be paid; a delay of up to 24 hours is possible, but the payment will be made. Timing is usually minutes to a few hours; see the Terms for details.
  • Deposit is normally on. If it is ever paused, the app shows the live status.

8Why we built this (our story)

This is a real story. The owner, MANDAL4482, started this platform because of his own bad experience as a Free Fire player.

  • He wanted to earn from playing Free Fire, so he tried many apps and websites and deposited money on them. Earlier he also played two-player games on a popular app.
  • On the apps he tried, the commission was so high that even a win often paid less than 1.5x. On the two-player games the profit was around 1.6x, and a loss meant the whole amount was gone.
  • Other apps had daily tournaments, but payments were delayed and the minimum withdrawal was very high, sometimes Rs 100 or Rs 200.
  • On one app, players kept joining but he could not withdraw. To test it, he put Rs 150 in his own account and Rs 150 in a friend’s account, and reached Rs 500. The app still did not pay. His trust was broken, and that anger is why he started FF Cash Battle.
  • He does not name those apps, because that would not be fair. He only says that in his own experience commission was very high, and that support was often hard to reach.

So FF Cash Battle was built on the opposite ideas: a small minimum deposit (Rs 5), a low flat withdrawal fee (Rs 2), no commission cut from your winnings, and an owner you can message directly.
