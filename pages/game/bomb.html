BOMB & GEMS

Cash Battle · win real prizes

Rules

5 × 5 board = 25 boxes. Some of them hide a bomb, the rest hide gems.

Bombs are hidden by the game server — nobody can see them.

A coin toss decides who plays first. Take turns opening one box: a gem is safe, a bomb means you lose 💣

Cash matches take a small cut; the rest goes to the winner.

Practice matches give no prize and are limited per day.

Cash matches are played only against real players. In a free practice match, if nobody is online, a bot joins — it is always marked BOT.
