4Deposits and Payment System

How Our Deposit System Works

FF Cash Battle currently uses UPI-based payment processing. Please understand that:

We do not currently operate a traditional third-party payment gateway or payment gateway checkout integration.

Our deposit verification works through an automated system called the Automated UPI Watcher Bot. The process is:

  • A user makes a payment using the supported payment method.
  • A payment notification is received through our designated Gmail notification channel.
  • Our Automated UPI Watcher Bot monitors the relevant payment notification.
  • The bot extracts available transaction information: transaction amount, transaction ID, payment date and time, and other available details.
  • The extracted information is checked and recorded in our database.
  • After successful verification, the eligible deposit amount is credited to the user’s account.

Deposits may occasionally require manual verification if the notification is delayed, incomplete, duplicated, or unavailable.

Important: An amount should not be considered successfully deposited until it has been verified and credited to the user’s account.
